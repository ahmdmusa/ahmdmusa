### Recent Projects & Designs

Here are some of the hardware and IC design blocks I've worked on recently (schematic, simulation, and full-custom layout):

- **7–10 GHz Programmable PLL**: Designed for RF applications (NTI Graduate Project).
- **PFD & Charge Pump**: Designed for a wide-range PLL and corner-verified on TSMC 65nm.
- **Bandgap Reference**: Designed on TSMC 65nm achieving 0.6 V, PSR −42 dB, and stable across −40 to 125 °C.
- **PTAT Temperature Sensor**: A BJT-based, low-power sensor designed on TSMC 180nm.
- **5 GHz Narrow-Band LNA**: Implemented using 65nm CMOS technology.
- **Two-Stage Miller OTA**: Achieved a DC gain of 68 dB, CMRR of 76 dB, and a slew rate of 5 V/µs.
- **[CMOS SRAM](https://github.com/ahmdmusa/lmplementation_Of_CMOS_SRAM)**: Transistor-level design and analysis of an SRAM cell.
- **[CMOS BCD Decoder](https://github.com/ahmdmusa/Simple-Binary-Coded-Decimal-Decoder-Implementation-using-CMOS-technology)**: Transistor-level implementation and full-custom layout on TSMC 65nm.
- **[AND Gate Layout](https://github.com/ahmdmusa/Design-AND--and-its-Layout--CMOS-Technology)**: Basic logic gate design with a DRC/LVS clean layout.
